The problem

Outbound got harder. Most teams are still doing it the old way.

Emails land in spam

Sending from your main domain without warm-up, rotation or monitoring burns your reputation, and your reps' emails go to spam with it.

SDRs are expensive and slow to ramp

A single SDR costs six figures a year once you add tools and management, and takes months to ramp. Most of their day goes to research, not selling.

Generic copy gets ignored

Buyers see dozens of templated pitches a week. Without real targeting and relevance, reply rates fall to zero.

FAQ

Questions we get asked

How fast can we get started?

Most campaigns go live about two weeks after kickoff. That covers strategy, domain and inbox setup, warm-up and list building.

Will this hurt my domain's reputation?

No. We send from separate domains we set up and manage for you. Your primary domain is never used for cold outreach.

Who writes the emails?

We do. Our team writes and tests every sequence, and you approve the messaging before anything goes out.

Where does the lead data come from?

We combine several B2B data providers, enrich each record with firmographic and buying-signal data, and verify every email address before sending.

What do I need to provide?

A kickoff call on your ICP, offer and past wins, access to a calendar for booking, and a point of contact for weekly check-ins. We handle the rest.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. We work month to month, because we expect results to keep you with us, not a contract.

Is cold email compliant?

We follow CAN-SPAM and GDPR best practices: relevant B2B targeting, clear sender identity, and an easy opt-out in every sequence.
